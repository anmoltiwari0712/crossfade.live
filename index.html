UNDER THE HOOD

Why regular extensions choke on UTube Music (and how v2 cheats physics)

Most volume extensions just fade one tab down to zero and pray. We actually capture the audio stream and run two decks in parallel.

01

Sneaky Second Deck

At page boot, CrossFade hooks Media Source Extensions in a lightweight ring buffer. Right before a track ends, it spins up a hidden Deck A with Song A’s tail—already synced to the millisecond.

02

Vocals Before Violence

Our Equal Power curve refuses to murder Song A’s last chorus. For 82% of the window, Song A stays crystal-clear (100% → 60%) while Song B creeps up underneath (0% → 48%), before a silky cosine/sine handoff.

03

Zero-Glitch Handoff

A pre-synced deck seek plus a 30ms Web Audio micro-ramp eliminates that annoying 1-second “mic click” when the player swaps to the next track. Pure butter.

PRIVACY PROMISE

Zero creeps. Zero analytics. Everything stays in your Chrome.

What we store (storage)

Only your 3 playback preferences: enabled, duration (1–12s), and curve (equalpower or linear), stored in your own Chrome Sync profile.

Why we touch music.youtube.com

Strictly to attach the Web Audio crossfade engine to playback tabs and display the live status badge inside the toolbar popup. Zero other websites are ever read.

What we never do

No telemetry, no external API calls, no cookies, no ads, no tracking scripts, and no selling of user data.
