CrossFade is the tiny Chrome extension that makes your songs overlap like they have met before. 1–12s customizable crossfade, dual audio decks, 0 awkward gaps, and 100% local audio processing.

Does YouTube Music have crossfade?

No. YouTube Music does not provide native crossfade or gapless transition settings on its web desktop player. CrossFade coordinates two audio decks using the W3C Web Audio API to blend tracks with 1–12s customizable overlap.

How CrossFade Works

  1. 01 Bring in the backup: CrossFade prepares the incoming track so it can join before the current one completely finishes.
  2. 02 The smooth handover: Song A gradually fades out while Song B fades in with Equal Power or Linear curves.
  3. 03 Keep the party going: Transitions happen automatically with zero awkward silence.
